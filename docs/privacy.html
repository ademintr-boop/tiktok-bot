Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Dark Stories Autopost is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account it is connected to. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), the tool accesses basic profile information, account statistics (followers, likes, video count), the list of the account's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, and permission to publish videos to that account.

How data is used

The data is used only to publish videos created by the tool to the owner's account and to measure how those videos perform, in order to choose future topics. Statistics are stored in the owner's private project files. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data of other users

The tool does not collect personal data of other TikTok users.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the stored statistics.
